# v2 color decisions

This short page is retained as a stable entry point. Detailed decision documents
are split by question so a palette role can link directly to the relevant rule.

- [Decision model](v2-decisions/README.md): what a justified decision contains
- [Evidence](v2-decisions/evidence.md): what each provenance class means
- [Candidate search](v2-decisions/candidate-search.md): selected, rejected, and
  next-passing candidates
- [Role policies](v2-decisions/role-policies.md): primary, interaction,
  foundation, boundary, focus, and destructive rules
- [Implementation status](v2-decisions/status.md): what is searched today and
  what remains a policy anchor
- [Craken integration](v2-decisions/craken-integration.md): why the sample, not
  the full tool, adopts the target design system

For a specific color, start from its `Why?` panel in the v2 page. Its provenance
links should open the relevant source or the exact role-policy section rather
than this index.
